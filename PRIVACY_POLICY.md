# Privacy Policy for QuickText AI

**Last Updated:** January 9, 2026

## Overview

QuickText AI is a browser extension that helps you transform text using AI. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

**In simple terms:** We only process text when you explicitly ask us to, and we don't store, track, or sell any of your data.

---

## What Data We Collect

### Data We Process (Temporarily)

| Data | When | Purpose |
|------|------|---------|
| Selected text | Only when you click an action button | To generate AI-powered text transformations |
| Language preference | When you change the language setting | To provide responses in your preferred language |

### Data We Do NOT Collect

- ❌ **No browsing history**
- ❌ **No personal information** (name, email, etc.)
- ❌ **No tracking or analytics**
- ❌ **No cookies for tracking**
- ❌ **No background data collection**
- ❌ **No keystrokes or form inputs**

---

## How We Use Your Data

1. **Text Processing Only When Requested**
   - Data is processed **only** when you explicitly select text and click an action (Rewrite, Polite, Professional, or Short)
   - We never scan or collect text without your direct action

2. **AI Generation**
   - Your selected text is sent to our AI service (powered by Groq) to generate the transformed text
   - The text is processed in real-time and **not stored** on any server

3. **No Background Collection**
   - The extension does not run in the background collecting data
   - It only activates when you explicitly use it

---

## Data Sharing

### We Do NOT Share Your Data

- ❌ We do **not** sell your data
- ❌ We do **not** share your data with advertisers
- ❌ We do **not** share your data with third parties for marketing

### Third-Party Services

We use **Groq** as our AI provider to process text transformations:

| Service | Purpose | Data Sent | Retention |
|---------|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Groq API | AI text generation | Only the text you select | Not stored (processed in real-time) |

Groq's privacy policy: [https://groq.com/privacy-policy/](https://groq.com/privacy-policy/)

---

## Data Storage

### Local Storage Only

- **Language preference**: Stored locally in your browser
- **No server-side storage**: We do not maintain user accounts or databases

### Data Retention

- **Text you submit**: Processed instantly, then discarded
- **No logs**: We do not log the content of your requests
- **No history**: We do not keep a history of your transformations

---

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- ✅ **Use the extension** without creating an account
- ✅ **Delete local data** by removing the extension
- ✅ **Control when data is sent** (only when you click an action)

---

## Security

We implement security measures to protect your data:

- 🔒 **HTTPS encryption** for all API communications
- 🔒 **No data persistence** on our servers
- 🔒 **Minimal permissions** requested by the extension

## Children's Privacy

QuickText AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
