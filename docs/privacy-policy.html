Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 9, 2026

QuickText AI ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our browser extension and related services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Text Data

When you use QuickText AI to process text:

  • Input Text: The text you select or paste for processing
  • Action Type: The action you request (rewrite, polite, professional, short)
  • Language Preference: Your selected output language
Important: Text is processed in real-time and is NOT stored permanently on our servers. It is only retained temporarily during processing (typically less than 30 seconds).

1.2 Usage Data

We collect anonymous usage statistics:

  • Number of requests per day (for quota management)
  • Action types used (aggregate, non-personal)
  • Error rates (for service improvement)

1.3 Account Data (Pro Users)

If you subscribe to QuickText AI Pro:

  • Email address (for account management)
  • Subscription status and billing history
  • Payment information (handled securely by Stripe)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process Text: Your input text is sent to our AI service (powered by Groq) to generate the requested output
  • Manage Quotas: Track daily usage limits for free users
  • Process Payments: Handle Pro subscriptions via Stripe
  • Improve Service: Analyze aggregate usage patterns to enhance features

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

3.1 Groq AI

Your text is processed using Groq's AI infrastructure. Groq processes text in real-time and does not retain user data. See Groq's Privacy Policy.

3.2 Stripe

Payment processing is handled by Stripe. We do not store your credit card information. See Stripe's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage

  • Local Storage: Preferences and authentication tokens are stored locally in your browser
  • Server Storage: We do NOT store the text you process
  • Analytics: Aggregate usage data is retained for up to 90 days

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure token-based authentication
  • Regular security audits
  • No permanent storage of processed text

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request information about data we hold about you
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Export: Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Opt-out: Uninstall the extension at any time to stop all data collection

7. Children's Privacy

QuickText AI is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes through the extension or by email (for Pro users).

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:
